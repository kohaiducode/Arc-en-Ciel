Access & Tourism

Ideally located on the middle corniche between Nice (8 km) and Monaco (6 km), Eze Village is a magical place planted in the azure, preserving an authentic atmosphere.

How to get here?

By Car

A8 highway exit 57 (La Turbie), take the direction of Eze Village. Parking near the tourist office.

By Plane

Nice Côte d'Azur international airport (25 km). Transfer by taxi or bus possible.

By Bus

Regional buses from Nice airport. Stop at 50m from the hotel.

By Train

Nearest train stations: Nice (8 km) or Monaco (7 km). Eze station at 2.2 km.
