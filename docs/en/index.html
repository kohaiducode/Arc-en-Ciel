Your enchanted getaway in Èze

Between land and sea, a haven of peace at the foot of one of the most beautiful villages in France.

Welcome to Arc en Ciel

Ideally situated on the middle corniche between Nice (8 Kms) and Monaco (6 Kms), Eze Village, a magical place planted in the azure, has preserved a special atmosphere of authenticity and tradition. The Hotel Arc en ciel is located at the foot of this village, one of the most beautiful in France. This establishment also features a bar (snack food) and a souvenir gift shop.

Discover Eze Village

An enchanting medieval village, Eze is an ideal holiday destination between Nice and Monaco. Perched at an altitude of 429 meters, it offers exceptional sea views. A perfect stopover that allows you to discover this sunny and picturesque town on foot.
